The studio

About

Octopup Studio was founded by a philosophy instructor who builds the practice tools used in their own logic courses. What began as classroom software has grown into a small studio with a broader aim: useful, carefully made apps for learning and community.

The name comes from our emblem, a puppy with octopus arms: approachable, curious, and able to handle many things at once. Look closely and it is also a piece of logic. Its arms branch like a decision tree, its face is the “because” sign ∵, and the first three branching points form the “therefore” sign ∴.
