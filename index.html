How we work

Approach

A few principles guide every app we make.

01

Learning by doing

Practice is broken into clear steps, with immediate feedback that explains each answer.

02

Built for real use

Our tools are shaped by the classroom and the communities that use them, not by trends.

03

Respect for privacy

We collect only what an app needs. Our logic apps need no account and keep progress on your own device.

The studio

About

Octopup Studio was founded by a philosophy instructor who builds the practice tools used in their own logic courses. What began as classroom software has grown into a small studio with a broader aim: useful, carefully made apps for learning and community.

The name comes from our emblem, a puppy with octopus arms: approachable, curious, and able to handle many things at once.
