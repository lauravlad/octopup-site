Meet Octopup

Octopup is half puppy, half octopus: friendly like a dog, clever like an octopus, with plenty of arms for juggling ideas. That's the kind of app we like to make.

Octopup Studio was started by a philosophy teacher who wanted practice tools students would actually enjoy using. The logic apps began in the classroom, and Swapfume grew out of a love of perfume.

  • ∴
    Learning, step by stepEvery logic exercise gives feedback, so you learn from each answer, right or wrong.
  • ♡
    Made to be sharedSame exercises for a whole class, and a community built on swapping, not selling.
  • ✓
    Simple and privateOur logic apps need no account and keep your progress on your own device.
